- Withdrew the initial paid Render recommendation, approximately US$27/month before extras. It was premature without comparing free providers and wrongly carried staging over from the issue title. The human's development/production correction controls the revised proposal.
- Checked current official documentation rather than assuming Vercel cannot host Django or Docker. [Vercel's Python runtime](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/runtimes/python) supports Django and Python 3.14; its [container runtime](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/runtimes/container) is beta on all plans. [Hobby](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby) is free for personal non-commercial use, with usage limits and no paid team collaboration features. Further research found [VCR image storage costs $0.10 per GB](https://vercel.com/docs/container-registry/limits-and-pricing), so the proposal uses native Python to avoid that additional storage charge. The [architecture proposal](../../workflow/specs/TECHNICAL-SPEC.md#architecture) makes provider-managed Python patching explicit. The first native proposal retained app-image checks and proposed no CLI; the subsequent simplification changes both choices for review.
- Proposed Render Free with Neon Free as the closest fit to the existing Docker baseline for a student demo, subject to the human accepting cold starts and quotas. [Render Free](https://render.com/docs/free) sleeps after 15 idle minutes and takes about a minute to wake; its own free PostgreSQL expires after 30 days. Render also limits service-initiated external traffic and can bill bandwidth/build overages when a payment method is present. [Neon Free](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans) offers 1 GB storage and 100 compute-unit hours per project per month; exhausted compute or transfer allowance suspends compute. [PostgreSQL 18 is generally available](https://neon.com/docs/changelog/2026-05-01). Neon Free requires an authenticated TLS connection over the public network, not assumed private networking. Provider capabilities were researched; no deployment was provisioned.
- Clarified that Vercel runs Django while a database provider stores PostgreSQL data. [Vercel's documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/postgres) directs new Postgres users to external Marketplace providers. Compared [Supabase Free](https://supabase.com/pricing), including its 500 MB database limit and [low-activity pausing](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/free-project-pausing); hosted PostgreSQL 18 support was not confirmed from its documentation. The human then selected Neon and Vercel Hobby, superseding the earlier Render Free recommendation.
